## Key Details

### SA-ARDT-STD-001 Management of Change
- Requester fills MOC form (QAS/032) with change type/description/duration
- Risk assessment per SA-PP-116 using QAS/041
- Quality Dept reviews, relevant authority approves
- Customer notification if contractually required
- Key Personnel changes require MOC

### SA-AV-1 Purchasing
- Classify items critical vs non-critical
- Vendor initial evaluation (on-site, remote, or inspection)
- Add to SA-AV-2 Approved Vendors list
- Re-evaluation: orders vs NCRs, <80% = removal
- Forms: QAS/101 Vendor Assessment, QAS/107 Vendor Reevaluation

### SA-PP-108 Design Control
- ADE plans design in ADesc portal
- Design in iBits 3D software
- Region Technology Manager reviews outputs
- US Global Design Support Team validates via RTA
- Field performance test for validation
- Material number assigned on acceptance

### SA-PP-112 Internal Audit
- At least every 12 months, no QMS part exceeds 12 months
- Auditors must be independent of audited area
- NCRs must be closed within 2 months
- Forms: QAS/012 Audit Plan, QAS/013 Report, QAS/014 NCR, QAS/050 Checklist

### SA-PP-115 Identification and Traceability
- Serial number + assembly number + job order number
- Unique job order per assembly in QAS/001
- Serial number assigned at final inspection
- Stickers: serial/size/type/IADC/tool joint/circulation
- Lost identification: NCR, quarantine, investigate

### SA-PP-116 Risk and Opportunities Control
- Severity 1-4 (Negligible to Catastrophic) x Probability 1-5 (Impossible to Frequent)
- RPN zones: 1-6 Low, 8-10 Medium, 12-20 Very High
- Form: QAS/041 Risk Assessment & Contingency Planning

### SA-PP-118 Management Review
- At least every 12 months
- 15 minimum input items including safety, audit results, customer satisfaction
- Outputs: QAS/037 Minutes with action items, timeframes, resources
- Quality objectives per department
