## Key Process Details

### SA-PP-1003 Brazing
- Team of 2: Torch Operator + Spin Operator
- At least one must be trained (SA-QP-6.1), other can be in training
- Kiln temp: 1100 F +/- 25 F
- Gas pressures: O2 8-15 PSI, Acetylene 6-12 PSI, N2 15 PSI
- Cerebro/torpedo housing must be protected before brazing
- Core heads: fill ID with ceramic fiber
- Steel bits >11"/12": clay over junk slot hard facing
- 16"+ bits: inswool over junk slots

### SA-PP-1004 Hard Facing (Steel Body Only)
- Preheat to ~700 F (use Tempil sticks 200/400/600/800)
- K1 Rope method: Techno Sphere GG wire through copper tube
- Flame Spray method: Superjet-S gun, TP3050 powder, O2 37 PSI, Acetylene 9 PSI
- Cool after application, remove graphite plugs, grind excess

### SA-PP-1013 LPT / Die Check
- NDT Level II required to interpret results
- NDT Level III approves procedures
- Vision exams: Jaeger #2 at 12", color contrast, annual renewal
- Written exam: 40 general + 20 specific questions, 80% pass
- Training: 12 hours + 210 hours method experience + 400 hours total NDT

### SA-PP-1014 Evaluation
- Decan and wash first (TEMCO Washer)
- API thread: pitting <1.5" length, <1/16" depth, <1/8" diameter
- Mud seal: no pitting >1/32" depth or >20% seal width
- Stand-off: 0.620"-0.66625" acceptable
- Blank Bond Lines: graded 1-3 (#3 = scrap)

### SA-PP-1017 Matrix Repair
- Heat 600-900 F (316-482 C)
- Eutectic 1249 powder, Torch Tip Eutectic B-3 C5
- Neutral flame, O2 20-25 PSI, Acetylene 7.5-10 PSI
- Grind pockets with diamond burr (not carbide)
- NCR required if discrepancies found after bit cools

### SA-PP-1018 Upper Section Replacement
- TIR within 0.005" at blades base and API thread
- Matrix bits: per drawing A40941-B
- Steel bits: per angle box drawing
- Runout check on OD and mud seal: within 0.010"

### SA-PP-1019 Cerebro
- 3 types: Torpedo (API Pin ID), Puck (1 cavity on shank), Force 2.0 (2 cavities)
- Torpedo cap torque: 50 ft-lbs
- 36+ specialized tools with material numbers
- Battery installation and LED confirmation required
