- Requestor, Job Title, Date
- Requestor Type: ARDT Employee / Customer / Both
- Duration: Permanent / Temporary + End Date
- Type of Change (dropdown)
- Description and Purpose (textarea)
- Resources Required (textarea)

### Section 2: RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITIES
- Allocation/reallocation required: Yes/No
- Person/Dept Responsible Before/After
- Person/Dept Authorized Before/After

### Section 3: RISK ASSESSMENT CHECK (7 Yes/No questions)
1. Impact health/safety?
2. Environmental impact?
3. Impact QMS/documents?
4. Impact product/service quality?
5. Impact equipment/tools?
6. Impact facilities/infrastructure?
7. Contract solution not covered?
- Risk Assessment Number + reviewed by Quality

### Section 4: APPROVAL
- Approval Authority (dropdown + Other)
- Approval Status: Approved/Rejected + signature
- Customer Approval Required: Yes/No
- Customer Name, Title, Contact, Approval Status

### Section 5: COMMUNICATION
- 9 name/job title slots
- Additional Comments/Lesson Learned

### Section 6: VERIFICATION
- MOC Completion Check: Yes/No
- MOC Impact on QMS (textarea)
- Quality Department Verified By (signature)
